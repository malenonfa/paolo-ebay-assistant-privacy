Privacy Policy – Paolo eBay Assistant Last updated: 21 September 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how Paolo eBay Assistant (“the Application”), a personal tool used by its operator to review and manage their own eBay seller activity, handles information accessed through eBay APIs. 1. Data accessed If authorized by the account holder, the Application may access eBay account and selling information needed for its functions, such as listings, inventory, offers, and order-related information. The Application is not intended for use by other eBay members. 2. Purposes of use Information is used only to support the operator’s personal review and management of their eBay selling activity, including reviewing listings, inventory, and related sales information. 3. Storage and security Access tokens and any eBay information retrieved by the Application will be handled with reasonable security measures and only in systems controlled by the operator or expressly selected for this project. No credentials or tokens should be published in this repository or shared publicly. The operator should update this policy if the actual storage or processing arrangements change. 4. Sharing and disclosure The Application does not sell eBay account information. Information is not intentionally disclosed to third parties except where necessary to operate the Application, comply with law, or where the account holder directs such disclosure. eBay processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. 5. Website hosting This privacy notice may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. For further information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. 6. Retention and deletion Information will be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above. The operator can stop the Application’s access by revoking its authorization through eBay account settings. Locally stored data and tokens should then be deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations. 7. Your choices and rights The eBay account holder may revoke the Application’s access at any time through eBay.
