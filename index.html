Privacy Policy

Paolo eBay Assistant

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Paolo eBay Assistant (“the Application”), a personal tool used by its operator to review and manage their own eBay selling activity, handles information accessed through eBay APIs.

1. Data accessed

If authorized by the account holder, the Application may access eBay account and selling information needed for its functions, such as listings, inventory, offers, and order-related information. The Application is intended for the operator’s personal use, not for use by other eBay members.

2. Purposes of use

Information is used to support the operator’s personal review and management of eBay selling activity, including reviewing listings, inventory, and related sales information.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and eBay information retrieved by the Application will be handled with reasonable security measures and only in systems controlled by the operator or expressly selected for this project. Credentials and tokens must not be published in this repository or shared publicly. This policy should be updated if the actual storage or processing arrangements change.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The Application does not sell eBay account information. Information is not intentionally disclosed to third parties except where necessary to operate the Application, comply with law, or where the account holder directs such disclosure. eBay processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Website hosting

This notice is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, for security and service operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

6. Retention and deletion

Information will be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above. The operator can stop the Application’s access by revoking its authorization through eBay account settings. Locally stored data and tokens should then be deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

7. Your choices and rights

The eBay account holder may revoke the Application’s access at any time through eBay. For questions about this policy or requests concerning information handled directly by the Application, contact the operator using the email address below.
